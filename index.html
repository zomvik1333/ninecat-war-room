      The goal

      Head to head categories is won one week at a time. Win 5 of the 9 cats and you win the week. So you do not need to be good at everything. You need to be clearly better than an average team in 5 or 6 cats and you can let 2 or 3 go on purpose. That is what a punt is. The board scores every player for the plan you pick and the left rail shows which cats you are winning after each pick.

      When someone takes your guy

      • First look for a same tier player with the same cats. The board groups tiers for you.
      • If that tier is empty, drop a tier but stay in your plan.
      • Only switch plans when the room has changed the market, for example 4 bigs gone in a row. The rail will show a switch banner when your roster leans toward another plan.
      • Never switch because of one missed pick. Switching costs picks you already made.
      • If the top pick has a high Survive number, take the one that will not come back first.

      How Auto keeps the strategy live

      • After every pick you mark, the board replays the rest of the draft 8 ways, once for each plan. Other teams are assumed to take the best player left by ADP, and you take the best fit for that plan.
      • Each replay gives your finished team a win chance in all 9 cats against an average team. The plan with the most expected cat wins is the one you follow.
      • Picks 1 and 2 are pure value. After pick 2 it commits to a plan.
      • It only switches when another plan beats the current one by 0.3 cats, or 0.4 after your 6th pick, so one missed player never flips your whole draft.
      • Every switch is written in the Strategy panel with what it gains and gives up.
      • The Best pick card is the player who gives you the highest projected cat count, not just the highest ranked name.
      • If you lock a plan yourself, it still tells you when another plan would win more cats.
      • Punt guard. A plan that leaves 4 or more cats as give ups is marked down, so Auto never drifts into a 4 cat punt.
      • Kawhi and Kyrie are never recommended before pick 40, Embiid never before pick 50.
      • From round 11 on, upside players get a bonus and injury risk counts half as much.

      How to run the mock, then the real draft

      • Set your draft slot and a starting plan. Balanced is fine for the first two picks.
      • On the Vercel site, tap Connect Yahoo once and sign in. After that every pick in the Yahoo draft room is marked here within a few seconds and goes to the right team. Nothing to tap.
      • Without Yahoo, type the name of each pick in the quick bar and press Enter. The board already knows whose turn it was, so every team's roster builds itself.
      • The Before your turn panel shows every team picking ahead of you, what they still need, and who they will probably take. The Teams tab shows all 10 rosters.
      • On your turn read the three cards on the left and the one line call under them.
      • After the mock, tap Reset draft and it is clean for the real one. Your picks save in this browser, so a refresh keeps them.
      • Stuck on a pick, paste the board situation to me in chat and I will make the call.
